Professional Summary

PhD physicist with 6+ years of hands-on experience in ultrafast spectroscopy, semiconductor nanomaterials synthesis (including thin-film deposition) and advanced materials characterization. Builds, aligns and troubleshoots complex laser-based and vacuum/controlled-atmosphere instrumentation.
