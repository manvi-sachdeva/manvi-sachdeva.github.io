I am an experimental physicist working on ultrafast spectroscopy and semiconductor nanomaterials. Currently I am an Institute Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Chemistry at IIT Roorkee. There I work on photoactive semiconductor materials for energy conversion and photodetection, covering thin-film preparation (sputtering, thermal evaporation), characterization and device-level performance.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the Institute of Nanoscience and Technology (INST), Mohali, under the supervision of Prof. Hirendra Nath Ghosh and Prof. Debabrata Patra. My thesis, Unravelling the Ultrafast Charge Carrier Dynamics in Near-Infrared Active Semiconductor Plasmonics and Their Heterostructures, studied how plasmonic hot carriers are generated, transferred and separated across nanocrystal interfaces.

My research interests include:

  • Hot-carrier dynamics in NIR-active semiconductor plasmonics (Cu2−xS, Cu2−xSe, CuxInyS)
  • Charge transfer across nanocrystal heterostructures and 2D interfaces
  • Femtosecond transient absorption and optical-pump terahertz-probe spectroscopy
  • Colloidal synthesis, thin-film deposition and interface engineering
  • Global/target analysis and kinetic modelling of time-resolved data

I am open to research positions worldwide, in both academia and industry.
