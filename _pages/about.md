I am an experimental physicist working on **ultrafast spectroscopy** and **semiconductor nanomaterials**. Currently I am an Institute Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Chemistry at [IIT Roorkee](https://iitr.ac.in/). There I work on photoactive semiconductor materials for energy conversion and photodetection, covering thin-film preparation (sputtering, thermal evaporation), characterization and device-level performance.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the [Institute of Nanoscience and Technology](https://inst.ac.in/) (INST), Mohali, under the supervision of Prof. Hirendra Nath Ghosh and Prof. Debabrata Patra. My thesis, _Unravelling the Ultrafast Charge Carrier Dynamics in Near-Infrared Active Semiconductor Plasmonics and Their Heterostructures_, studied how plasmonic hot carriers are generated, transferred and separated across nanocrystal interfaces.

My research interests include:

- Hot-carrier dynamics in NIR-active semiconductor plasmonics (Cu<sub>2−x</sub>S, Cu<sub>2−x</sub>Se, Cu<sub>x</sub>In<sub>y</sub>S)
- Charge transfer across nanocrystal heterostructures and 2D interfaces
- Femtosecond transient absorption and optical-pump terahertz-probe spectroscopy
- Colloidal synthesis, thin-film deposition and interface engineering
- Global/target analysis and kinetic modelling of time-resolved data

I am open to research positions worldwide, in both academia and industry.
